About Star Birthdays

In Hindu tradition, your star birthday (Nakshatra birthday) is celebrated when the Moon returns to the same nakshatra (lunar constellation) and tithi (lunar day) in which you were born, in the same paksha (waxing or waning phase). This is considered more auspicious than the regular solar calendar birthday.

Note: This calculator uses astronomical calculations based on traditional Vedic principles. The dates are approximations. For precise religious observances, please consult with a local pandit or priest.
