Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2025

Introduction

Star Birthday Calculator ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy explains how we handle your information when you use our website.

Information We Collect

Personal Information You Provide

When you use our contact form, we may collect:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Subject of your inquiry
  • Message content

Calculator Data

When you use our star birthday calculator, you may enter:

  • Birth date
  • Birth time (optional)
  • Location coordinates (latitude and longitude)

Important: All calculator data is processed entirely in your browser. We do not collect, store, or transmit any of your birth information or calculation results to our servers or any third parties.

How We Use Your Information

Contact Form Data

Information submitted through our contact form is used solely to:

  • Respond to your inquiries
  • Provide customer support
  • Improve our services based on your feedback

Calculator Data

Your calculator data is never transmitted to our servers. All calculations happen locally in your web browser using JavaScript. When you close your browser or navigate away from the calculator page, your data is automatically cleared.

Data Storage and Security

Client-Side Processing: Our calculator operates entirely on the client side. Your birth date, time, and location data never leave your device.

No Cookies: We do not use cookies to track your activity or store your personal information.

No Analytics: We do not use third-party analytics tools that track your browsing behavior.

Third-Party Services

Our website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may collect technical information such as IP addresses for security and service provision purposes. Please refer to GitHub's Privacy Statement for more information.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Know what personal data we hold about you (contact form submissions only)
  • Request deletion of your personal data
  • Opt-out of any future communications
  • Update or correct your information

To exercise these rights, please contact us through our contact page.

Children's Privacy

Our service is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new privacy policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date at the top.

Disclaimer

Our calculator is provided for educational and informational purposes only. The calculations are based on astronomical formulas and Vedic principles but should not replace consultation with qualified astrologers or religious advisors for important ceremonies.
